AMOLED Agenda privacy

The Android app connects directly to Google Calendar and Todoist with read-only permissions. OAuth credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore. Last-success agenda content is stored in app-private, no-backup storage on your device. There is no application account backend, analytics, advertising, or enabled crash reporting.

Disconnect in the app removes that provider’s local credentials and cached content. Revoke the app in Google or Todoist account settings to withdraw provider consent. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove all local app data.

This static GitHub Pages site serves public client metadata, Android verified-link information and these pages only. It never exchanges OAuth codes or stores provider tokens. If Android does not open a verified callback link, the browser and hosting infrastructure receive the callback URL (which can include a short-lived authorization code and state). The page removes its query from browser history; this cannot erase infrastructure request records. Do not share callback URLs. Hosting is subject to GitHub’s privacy policy.

Google and Todoist process account authorization and API requests under their own policies. No live account validation is claimed by this site.
